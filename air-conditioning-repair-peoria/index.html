What a repair visit can involve

A repair visit generally starts with a discussion of the symptoms and a check of the system. The technician can look for the cause rather than relying only on what the thermostat or vents seem to indicate. Once the issue is identified, you can talk through the repair needed and what to expect before work proceeds.

Before calling, use this short checklist:

  • Note whether the unit runs, stops, or will not start.
  • Check and record the thermostat setting.
  • Describe any new sound, odor, or change in airflow.
  • Make a clear path to the indoor equipment and outdoor unit, if accessible.
  • Gather any details you have about recent work or earlier symptoms.

Avoid repeatedly switching the system on and off to see if it will recover. Don't remove covers, handle wiring, or attempt a repair that could damage equipment or put you at risk. If there is a burning smell or another immediate safety concern, stop using the system and explain that concern when you call.
