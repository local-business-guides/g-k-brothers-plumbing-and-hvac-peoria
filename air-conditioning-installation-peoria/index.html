What to expect from an installation discussion

A contractor will need to understand the existing system and the work being considered. The discussion may cover the equipment to be replaced, where the current components are located, and how the system connects with the home. Before work is planned, ask what the proposed scope includes, what preparation is needed, and how the system will be checked after installation.

Use this checklist before making contact:

  • Write down the make or model information if it is easy to find.
  • Note which rooms you want the system to serve.
  • Describe any recurring comfort concerns without assuming their cause.
  • Identify where the indoor equipment and outdoor unit are located.
  • Gather questions about scheduling, access, and the work area.

Don't choose equipment based only on a label or a guess about what the home needs. Avoid making changes to wiring, refrigerant lines, or other system parts before a contractor has reviewed the setup. Clear questions can help you understand the proposed work and decide how to proceed.
